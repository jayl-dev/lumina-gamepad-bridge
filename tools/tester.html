ESP Gamepad Tester

Sends input to the Lumina ESP32-S3 board over USB serial, exactly as Lumina would, and reads back what macOS reports through the browser Gamepad API. Use Chrome or Edge. Quit Lumina first, because it holds the serial port exclusively.

1. Send to board

Not connected
Left stick 0, 0
Right stick 0, 0
Keyboard: WASD left stick · IJKL right stick · arrows D-pad · 1/3 LT/RT · button keys shown on each button. Hold a key to hold the input.

2. What macOS reports

Chrome only exposes a gamepad after one of its buttons is pressed. Connect the board, then press A.
